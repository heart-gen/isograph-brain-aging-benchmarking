## Reading guide

- A bare negative usage correlation is near-vacuous evidence of a switch (compositional closure; see `../switch_orthogonal_confirm/GLOBAL_NULL.md`), so the rate must be read against the matched null, never against zero.
- The two backgrounds answer different questions: all non-driver switch pairs is the anchored arm's universe and the primary comparison; the same-gene background controls for gene but is small.
- n = 12 donors, half AD, DLPFC against GTEx cortical regions; no per-sample age, so nothing here is a signed usage-vs-age replication.
