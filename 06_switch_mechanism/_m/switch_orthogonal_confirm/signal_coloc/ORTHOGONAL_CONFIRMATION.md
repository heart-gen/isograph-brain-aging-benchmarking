# Orthogonal long-read confirmation of genetically anchored switch pairs — signal-level coloc

Generated by `switch_orthogonal_confirm.py --mode anchored --events signal` (seed 13, 2000 draws).

## Question

Do the genetically anchored (sQTL-colocalizing) IsoGraph switch pairs behave like switches in orthogonal ONT long-read DLPFC data?

The anchored events here come from the signal-level colocalization layer (`coloc.susie`, all-introns arm): each tissue where a nomination's sQTL reaches the call, resolved to the transcripts that splice that intron. It is a separate arm from the eCAVIAR (CLPP) events behind S-real-8 and the two are not pooled. Every call still rests on GTEx short-read sQTL alone. This scores the *specific anchored transcript pair* -- not the gene -- on an independent platform, lab, cohort and quantifier (Aguzzoli-Heberle 2024 NBT; Bambu; Zenodo 8180677; n=12 DLPFC BA9/46).

## Result

- 264 anchored pairs over 21 anchored genes; 108 detected in long-read.
- **62 are switch-like** (both isoforms detected, usage negatively rank-correlated).
- **15 of 21 genes** have at least one orthogonally confirmed anchored pair.
- Restricting to pairs whose *anchored* isoform reaches 0.05 mean isoform fraction: 35 of 90 pairs, and **11 of 21 genes**.

Against an abundance-matched background of 30881 non-anchored IsoGraph switch pairs, the anchored switch-like rate is **0.574** versus a matched-null mean of 0.243 (95% null interval 0.167-0.324, empirical two-sided p = 0.0005, n = 108 testable pairs).

Matching is on the abundance decile of the better-expressed member, because at n=12 that is what governs whether a usage correlation is estimable at all.

Restricted to the 50 pairs whose anchored isoform is itself usably expressed, the rate is **0.700** against a matched null of 0.301 (p = 0.0005).

### The set-level result does not transfer to every gene

6 of 21 genes fail the abundance qualification entirely (DGKZ, KLC1, NDUFAF7, PTPRN, RERE, WIPI2): their anchored isoform never reaches 0.05 mean isoform fraction in long read, so no usage correlation computed on it is interpretable. Read the confirmation at the level of the anchored *set*, where the matched comparison is made, and not locus by locus.

## Cross-tissue exceptions (reviewed, not pooled)

UNC13A: the colocalizing tissue has no IsoGraph switch pair for the gene, so the pair was taken from the regions where the colocalizing junction's transcripts ARE switch-pair members (`cross_tissue_regions` in the event table). That changes the reading: a switch-like result says these transcripts behave like a switch in long-read DLPFC, not that a tissue-matched switch is genetically anchored. The pairs are scored by the same code but kept out of the set-level comparison above and out of its background.

- 24 pairs; 0 detected; **0 switch-like**; 0 of 6 at usable abundance (max anchored isoform fraction 0.999).

## How to read a negative

A pair that is *detected but not switch-like* is the informative case: the transcripts exist on an orthogonal platform, so the failure is not one of annotation or of technology. Check `t1_mean_if` / `t2_mean_if` before concluding anything -- a pair whose members sit at a fraction of a percent of the gene's output carries no usable usage signal in n=12 samples, and its correlation is tie-breaking noise rather than evidence against the switch. The abundance-matched comparison above is what separates those two readings at the set level; at the level of one gene, report the fractions.
